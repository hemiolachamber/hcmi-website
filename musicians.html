Our Musicians

Madison Jones

Violin

A native of Lexington and a veteran of both the CKYO and UK Symphony, Madison Jones began her violin studies at age five. Her professional experience includes guest appearances with the Chamber Orchestra of Louisville and Cave Run Symphony. She currently serves as Assistant Concertmaster of the Lexington Philharmonic, is a member of the Lexington Chamber Orchestra, maintains a private studio, and performs as a freelance musician.

Ethan Young

Cello

Ethan Young is a cellist in the Lexington Philharmonic and master cello teacher with the University of Kentucky String Project, as well as an instructor with the Central Kentucky Youth Orchestra and a teaching artist for the Lexington Philharmonic. His musical life spans classical, rock, pop, country, funk, and jazz, with a passion for playing with friends and sharing music with the community.

Chase Miller

Clarinet

Chase Miller is a professional clarinetist and educator in Central Kentucky. He has taught at Transylvania University, Centre College, and Asbury University and serves as program director of CKYO MusicWorks. He has been recognized with the Kentucky Governor's School for the Arts Marlene M. Helm Alumni Achievement Award and participated in South Arts' Emerging Leaders of Color program.

Chris Mears

Guitar and Vocals

Chris Mears is a Lexington-based film and television composer and music editor whose musical path has included bands, recording projects, touring, songwriting, and performance. His work reflects a continuing fascination with musical discovery and the possibilities within each new composition.

Ensemble

Formosa Trio

The Formosa Trio, featuring Taiwanese musicians Tze-Ying Wu, viola; Pei-San Chiu, flute; and Chen-Yu Huang, harp, has performed actively together since 2015. Dedicated to expanding and promoting repertoire for this distinctive instrumentation, the ensemble regularly commissions composers to create new works and also creates its own arrangements ranging from classical compositions to Taiwanese folk songs.

The trio has toured throughout the United States and Taiwan and appeared at flute, harp, and viola conventions and festivals. In 2018, the ensemble released its first album.

Tze-Ying Wu is Associate Professor of Viola and String Education at the University of Kentucky. Pei-San Chiu is Adjunct Associate Professor of Music in Flute at Indiana University, and Chen-Yu Huang is a harp specialist with Fort Bend Independent School District in Texas and a former Assistant Professor of Harp at Michigan State University.

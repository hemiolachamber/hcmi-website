Dr. Tze-Ying Wu

Founder & Artistic Director

Dr. Tze-Ying Wu is Associate Professor of Viola and String Education at the University of Kentucky, where her work reflects her core passions for chamber music, string pedagogy, and building communities through music and outreach. As a performer and educator, she believes that music can create meaningful connections among musicians and between artists and the communities they serve.

As Director of the UK String Project, Dr. Wu works to make high-quality string education accessible to pre-college students while fostering the next generation of music educators. Through the project's mentorship-based model, university string students gain hands-on teaching experience with young musicians, creating a community in which teaching, learning, and mentorship are closely connected.

Dr. Wu received her graduate degrees from the Indiana University Jacobs School of Music and maintains an active performing career. She has participated extensively in orchestral and chamber music performances throughout Central Kentucky and beyond.
